PARKS PAL

Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026 · Applies to the Parks Pal mobile app (iOS & Android)

Parks Pal is a national park passport — it tracks the parks you've visited, the trips you log or plan, and the badges you earn along the way. This page explains, plainly, what the app collects, why, and how you stay in control of it.

01 What We Collect

  • Account info — the email address you sign up with, used only to authenticate you.
  • Park & trip data — parks you mark visited, wishlisted, or planned; trip dates, activities, trail and wildlife entries, and any notes you write.
  • Photos — images you choose to attach to a trip log, uploaded only when you add them.
  • Profile details — your display name, explorer style, avatar, park goal, and unit preference (miles/kilometers).

We don't collect location data, contacts, or anything from your device beyond what you enter into the app yourself.

02 How We Use It

Solely to power your Parks Pal passport: tracking your progress toward all 63 parks, showing your trip history and stats, calculating badges, and syncing your data across your own devices. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03 Where It Lives

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and file-storage provider, inside a project only Parks Pal's backend can access. Every row is scoped to your account with row-level security, and trip photos are served through short-lived private links only you can generate — no one else, signed in or not, can read your trips, photos, or profile.

DataStored whereRetention
Account & profileSupabase (Postgres)Until you delete your account
Trips, trails, wildlife logsSupabase (Postgres)Until you delete the trip or account
Trip photosSupabase Storage (private)Until you delete the photo, trip, or account

04 Your Control

You can edit or delete any trip, park status, or profile detail at any time from within the app. Signing out does not delete your data — it's waiting for you next time.

Deleting your account is permanent and immediate: open Settings → Delete Account in the app. It removes your profile, every trip and photo, and all progress — there's no recovery window, so only confirm once you're sure.

05 Children's Privacy

Parks Pal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

06 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date above and the in-app copy under Settings → Privacy Policy.

Questions about your data? Reach us at parkpal.support@gmail.com.
