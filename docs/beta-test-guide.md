# Parks Pal — What to Test

A quick guide for beta testers. You don't need to test everything — pick the areas that match how you'd actually use the app, and tell us where it felt confusing, slow, or wrong. Rough edges are exactly what we're looking for.

## How to send feedback

For each issue, note: **which screen**, **what you did**, **what you expected**, **what happened** (a screenshot helps a lot), and your **phone model + iOS version**. "This confused me" is valid feedback — you don't need to prove it's a bug.

---

## 1. First impressions (do this first, before reading the rest)

- Open the app cold. Without any guidance, could you tell what it does and what to tap first?
- Was signing up / logging in smooth? Anything you hesitated on?
- Was the onboarding (explorer style / goal) clear, and did skipping it work?

## 2. Trail and animal accuracy

This is the one we can't check ourselves — **if you've been to a park, please compare it to what you remember.** Pick 1–3 parks you know well.

**Trails** (Parks tab → a park → Trails → a trail)
- Do the **distance**, **elevation gain**, and **difficulty** feel right for the trail you actually hiked?
- Is the **estimated time** realistic?
- Does the **elevation profile** shape match the real trail (steady climb, dip and climb back, loop, etc.)?
- Are the **best season**, **trail tip**, and **permit / closure notes** correct? These change over time, so tell us if anything is outdated.
- Is the **park itself** correct on each trail (right trail, right park)?
- Are any **iconic trails missing** from a park you know?

**Animals** (Parks tab → a park → Animal Compendium)
- Is each animal actually **found in that park**? Any that shouldn't be there?
- Do the **rarity** labels (Common / Uncommon / Rare) feel fair for how often you'd really see them?
- Are the **best time of day**, **best season**, and **where to look** helpful and correct?
- Are the **viewing tips** sensible and safe (keep distance, don't approach)?
- Any **iconic animals missing**?

**Found something wrong?** Tap the small flag icon in the top-right of any trail or animal page, pick what's off, and send it — reports go straight to our weekly data review. Also check the small **"Verified <month>"** note at the bottom of each page: it shows when permit and closure info was last checked, and warns you when it may be out of date.

**Park info** — description, state, year established, acreage, and location on the map.

## 3. Logging and managing trips

- **Log a trip** (the + button): try a one-day trip and a multi-day trip. Was the day-by-day step understandable?
- **Plan a trip** for a future date, then **mark it completed** later.
- Add **activities, trails hiked, wildlife spotted, weather, notes, and a rating**. Was picking trails/animals from the park's list easy? Did it miss ones you wanted?
- Add **photos** (up to 3 per trip). Do they show up right after saving? Tap a photo in the form to write a short **caption** (80 characters); the small X removes it. Do captions show under the photos in the Photo Album, and do they survive editing the trip?
- **Edit** and **delete** a trip. Does everything you entered come back correctly?
- Did trip **miles and elevation totals** add up sensibly?
- **Switch units** (Settings → Units, miles ↔ kilometers) and check distances everywhere.

## 4. Photos and Photo Album

- Look for the small **photo icon** on trip cards — does it appear only when a trip has photos?
- Open **Profile → Photo Album**. Do the photo stacks look right, are they easy to tap, and does the layout fit your screen?
- Tap a stack — are the photos centered and readable?
- Does anything get **cut off, off-center, or overlapping** on your phone size?

## 5. Progress, badges, and profile

- Mark a park as **visited**, add it to your **bucket list**, and check the **map** and progress ring update.
- Check **Passport**, **Collection** (badges), **Stats**, and **Bucket List**.
- Do **badges** unlock when you'd expect? Are the descriptions clear? (Log a trip with a spotted Island Fox at Channel Islands, Atlantic Puffin at Acadia, Gray Wolf at Yellowstone, California Condor at Pinnacles, or Manatee at Everglades to unlock the bonus pals.)
- Mark **Angels Landing** (Zion) or **Half Dome** (Yosemite) as completed to unlock bonus profile backgrounds (Profile → the photo icon on the header). Locked ones show a lock and tell you what to earn.
- Change your **profile avatar and background**. Do locked pals explain how to unlock them?

## 6. Navigation and general feel

- **Back button:** from each screen you reach, does back take you where you came from? Try Profile → a trip → back, and Bucket List → a park → back.
- Any screen that felt **slow, jumpy, or froze**?
- Any text that's **too small, cut off, or hard to read**? Any button that's hard to tap?
- Do the **colors, illustrations, and mascot** feel consistent across screens?
- Anything that **looked broken or empty** (blank areas, missing images)?

## 7. Connection and reliability

- Turn on **airplane mode**, log a new trip, then turn it off. The trip should appear right away with a small cloud icon and sync on its own after you reconnect. Did it?
- Still in airplane mode, try **editing a trip, deleting a trip, marking a trail complete, adding a park to your bucket list, changing your pal or background, and reporting a trail problem**. Each should work instantly, and a small "Offline" note should appear at the top. After you reconnect, do all of them sync, with a "Back online" message?
- With no connection, can you still browse parks, trails, and animals?
- **Force-quit and reopen the app while still offline.** Do your trips, visited parks, bucket list and profile all still show?
- Anything that looks like it **undid itself** after reconnecting (a change reverting, a deleted trip coming back, something showing twice)? That's exactly what we want to hear about.

## 8. Account

- **Forgot password** flow, and **log out / log back in**.
- **Delete account** (Settings) — does it clearly warn you, and does it work?
- Are the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms** easy to find and readable?

---

## Known limitations (no need to report)

- Photos on older trips may look blank when offline (their links expire after a week and refresh when you're back online).
- Tapping **Back on the "+" Log a Trip chooser screen** may do nothing.
- Some trail and permit details are **time-sensitive** and may lag reality — but do tell us when they're wrong.

## A few questions to answer at the end

1. What was the **most confusing** moment?
2. What did you **enjoy most**?
3. What's **one thing you expected the app to do** that it didn't?
4. Would you use this to **track your real trips**? Why or why not?
